## 🔥 [Planoramic](https://github.com/archipelagoing/planoramic)

**an ambient planning surface for the home**

Planoramic turns Fire TV into a shared household calendar that is designed to be understood from across the room.

i'm currently exploring how **Fire TV, Alexa+, Amazon Bedrock, Google Calendar, voice, and shared state** can work together as one multimodal experience.

**voice for intent · TV for understanding · remote for exploration · phone for configuration**

## 🧅 [SituatiONION](https://github.com/archipelagoing/Situationion)

**studying how relational and situation-sensitive information changes across transformer depth**

SituatiONION is an interpretability project centered on GPT-2 XL. It uses controlled paraphrase/counterfactual experiments, layerwise probing, geometric analysis, and representation comparisons to investigate what information is accessible at different transformer layers.

current questions include:

- how paraphrases and counterfactuals separate across depth
- where relational information becomes decodable
- how different token/readout choices change what we observe
- whether apparent mid-layer structure survives stronger controls
- how representations generalize to unfamiliar compositions

## ☁️ [PCA Cloud](https://github.com/archipelagoing/PCACloud)

**turn your spreadsheet into clouds**

an interactive browser-based tool for exploring high-dimensional datasets using **PCA and t-SNE**.

upload a CSV and explore its projected structure as an interactive 3D cloud, compare dimensionality-reduction methods, inspect explained variance, and save visualizations.

**[open PCA Cloud →](https://archipelagoing.github.io/PCACloud/)**

## 🧬 [EvoNashMTL](https://github.com/archipelagoing/EvoNashMTL)

**game theory + evolutionary dynamics for multi-task learning**

an experimental extension of Nash-MTL that combines:

- Nash bargaining for stepwise task fairness
- replicator-style dynamics for longer-term task adaptation
- gradient-based multi-task optimization

the project asks whether evolutionary scheduling can help prevent tasks from being persistently neglected during shared-model training.

---

## 🌐 [CoalitionMind](https://github.com/archipelagoing/CoalitionMind)

**what if a decision-making system behaved more like a coalition than a single agent?**

a small experimental cognitive architecture where multiple internal agents compete and cooperate over a shared concept graph.

agents representing reactive, habitual, deliberative, and social strategies propose actions, while a coalition policy determines the system's final behavior.

---

## 🧴 [Tenue](https://github.com/archipelagoing/Tenue)

**a social identity layer built around fragrance ownership and presence**

Tenue explores how physical fragrance ownership, wear history, and communities around individual fragrances could form a persistent social network.

built around:

- ownership
- presence
- fragrance communities
- cultural activity
- personal wear history
